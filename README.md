# SANGAM
A community platform connecting youth with rural knowledge holders through meaningful experiences.
# 🌱 SANGAM – Where Generations Unite

### Where Wisdom Meets Innovation, and Generations Grow Together.

## About the Project

SANGAM is a technology-enabled platform that connects young people with elderly individuals, farmers, rural women and artisans through two-way intergenerational learning.

It creates opportunities for youth to learn traditional wisdom, cultural heritage and practical skills from older generations, while helping elders gain digital knowledge, awareness of government schemes and understanding of modern innovations from youth.

## Problem Statement

India's rich heritage, traditional wisdom and indigenous skills are gradually becoming unknown to younger generations. At the same time, many youth face stress, loneliness and pressure in modern life, missing valuable guidance and human connections.

Elderly people often remain disconnected from useful modern innovations, digital tools and government schemes. The growing generation gap limits mutual learning and emotional connection.

## Our Solution

SANGAM aims to bridge this generation gap through shared learning experiences, digital inclusion and community participation.

## Key Features

- Wisdom Circles – Learning through stories and life experiences.
- Nature Connect – Exploring farming, gardening and nature.
- Skills of Our Roots – Preserving traditional arts and local skills.
- Digital Saathi – Helping elders understand smartphones, digital services, online safety and government schemes.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (inline interaction)
- Google Forms for community registration


## How It Works

1. Register – Youth and knowledge partners register through the Google Form.
2. Connect – Participants can be connected based on their interests and skills. Automated matching is a proposed future feature.
3. Experience – Participants take part in shared learning activities.
4. Feedback – Participants can share their feedback. An integrated feedback management system is planned for future development.

## Expected Impact

- Preservation of India's traditional knowledge.
- Digital inclusion of elderly people.
- Stronger connections between generations.
- Practical learning opportunities for youth.
- Support for farmers, rural women and artisans.
