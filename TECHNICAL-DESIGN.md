# SANGAM – Technical Design Document

## 1. Project Overview
SANGAM is a web-based platform designed to connect young people with elderly individuals, farmers, rural women and artisans through two-way intergenerational learning.

The platform encourages youth to learn traditional knowledge and practical skills from older generations, while helping elders understand digital tools and modern innovations.

## 2. Technology Stack
- HTML5 – Website structure.
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive layout.
- JavaScript – Basic website interactions.
- Google Forms – Community registration and response collection.
- GitHub – Source code hosting and project documentation.

## 3. System Architecture
The current prototype follows a simple client-side architecture.

1. Users access the SANGAM website through a web browser.
2. HTML provides the structure and content.
3. CSS controls the visual design and responsive layout.
4. JavaScript supports basic interactions.
5. The registration button connects users to an external Google Form.
6. Registration responses are collected through Google Forms.

## 4. Main Modules

### Wisdom Circles
Encourages sharing of stories, life experiences and traditional wisdom.

### Nature Connect
Promotes learning about farming, gardening and nature.

### Skills of Our Roots
Highlights traditional arts, crafts and local skills.

### Digital Saathi
Encourages youth to help elders understand smartphones, digital services, online safety and government scheme information.

## 5. User Flow
- Register: Participants submit their details through the registration form.
- Connect: The proposed model connects participants according to their interests.
- Experience: Participants take part in shared learning activities.
- Feedback: Participants share their experiences and suggestions.

## 6. Data and Privacy
The current prototype uses Google Forms for registration. It does not currently include a custom backend, user authentication or an integrated database. Personal information should be collected only with appropriate consent and handled responsibly.

## 7. Current Implementation Status
The project currently includes a responsive website, information about learning activities and an external community registration form.

Participant matching, partner verification, feedback management and reward mechanisms are proposed future features and are not yet fully implemented.

## 8. Future Enhancements
- Interest-based participant matching.
- Verified community knowledge partners.
- Digital learning resources.
- Structured feedback and reward mechanisms.
- Expansion to rural communities.

## 9. Conclusion
SANGAM uses simple web technologies to create a platform for mutual learning, digital inclusion and cultural preservation. Its long-term vision is to bring generations together through shared knowledge and community participation.
