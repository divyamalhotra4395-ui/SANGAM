About SANGAM

In today's fast-moving world, connections between generations and nature are gradually decreasing. Traditional knowledge, local skills and valuable life experiences are often left unexplored.

SANGAM aims to bridge this gap by bringing young people together with elderly individuals, farmers, rural women and artisans through meaningful real-world experiences.

Our initiative promotes intergenerational learning, nature engagement, cultural preservation, social connection and additional earning opportunities for local knowledge partners.

SANGAM believes in two-way learning. Young people gain traditional wisdom, farming practices and cultural skills from older generations, while helping elders develop digital confidence through smartphones, online safety and digital services.

🚀 How Our Platform Works in Real Life

1. Register

Youth participants and local knowledge partners register through our online platform.

2. Connect

Participants are matched according to their interests, skills and available activities.

3. Experience

Youth meet knowledge partners through planned activities such as farming, gardening, storytelling and traditional art.

4. Feedback & Rewards

Participants share feedback. Knowledge partners can receive suitable payments for their time, skills and contribution.
