About Roots & Reconnect

In today's fast-moving world, connections between generations and nature are gradually decreasing. Traditional knowledge, local skills and valuable life experiences are often left unexplored.

Roots & Reconnect aims to bridge this gap by bringing young people together with elderly individuals, farmers, rural women and artisans through meaningful real-world experiences.

Our initiative promotes intergenerational learning, nature engagement, cultural preservation, social connection and additional earning opportunities for local knowledge partners.
